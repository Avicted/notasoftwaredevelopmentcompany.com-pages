Questions nobody asks

FAQ: Can I hire notasoftwaredevelopmentcompany? Do you do software development? What is on the front page? Answers to questions nobody has asked.

Can I hire you?

No. We are not a software development company. This is not modesty. It is the business model.

Do you do software development?

We do software. We do development. We do not put them together and charge money for it.

Then why do you know so much about software?

Hobby. Some people knit. We write C and compile it to WebAssembly.

What is that on the front page?

A spectrogram of a 56k modem connecting: the dial tone, the number being dialled, the ringback, the 2100 Hz answer tone, the V.8 negotiation, the line probing and the hiss of the two modems training. Time runs left to right, pitch bottom to top. Click anywhere and you will hear it, at a quarter volume; your browser insists on the click. The numbers in the corner are how long each frame takes to draw. Click the picture to hide them.

None of it is a recording. The sound is synthesized and run through an FFT, live in your browser, by freestanding C with no libc, compiled with plain clang. It is the most engineered thing we have never sold.

Can I get an estimate?

Yes: never. It is the only estimate we have ever given, and it has always been accurate.

What technologies do you use?

Linux, containers, REST APIs, Hugo, nginx and a text file. The full list of things we are experts at, allegedly, is on the front page.

Do you have any products?

We have things that are not products. The font converter turns your text into box-drawing characters. It is free, because nobody would pay for it.

How do I contact you?

Press the contact button on the front page. You will get an error. That is the contact.
